How to Brew Your DIY Tube

1

Heat 250ml milk: Simmer whole milk in a small saucepan until small bubbles form around the edge (do not boil).

2

Whisk the contents: Uncork your tube and whisk the chocolate shavings and cocoa powder gently on low flame for 90 seconds until silky thick.

3

Top & Enjoy: Pour into your favorite cozy mug and float the dehydrated marshmallows on top.
